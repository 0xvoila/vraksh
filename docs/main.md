## Meaning of relations

1. nsub
   1. nominal subject
   2. subject of the verb
   3. 
2. iobj
   1. indirect object
   2. indirect object of the verb
3. dobj
   1. direct object
   2. direct object of the verb
4. prep
   1. preposition
   2. preposition of the verb
5. pobj
   1. preposition object
   2. object of the preposition
6. det
   1. determiner
   2. determiner of the noun
7. advmod (adverb rel)
   1. adverb modifier 
   2. word that modifies the verb
8. advcl (adverb rel)
   1. adverb clause 
   2. a clause is present to modify (add details to ) the main verb clause
   3. Without advcl also, sentence is complete
      1. Examples
         1. I visit temple because I feel in peace 
         2. I eat fruits if they are sweet
9. relcl (noun modifer)
   1.  Relation clause modifier
   2.  Unlike advcl, which modifies verb i.e. explain more about action, relcl modifies noun
   3.  Examples
       1.  I am working on a project that (I love) (relcl)
       2.  
10. amod (adjective rel)
   1.  Adjective modifier
   2.  Sit next to noun
       1.  Examples
           1.  Amit is a good(noun) boy(amod)
11. acomp (adjective rel)
    1.  Adjective complement
    2.  Sit next to linking verb ( is , to be , become etc)
    3.  Examples
        1.  Amit is(verb) good(acomp)
12. ccomp (object rel)
    1.  clipped complement
    2.  When object is a clause with its own subject and verb
    3.  If you remove ccomp component then sentence become incomplete
    4. Examples
       1. The programmer says (main verb) that (he is written the script) (ccomp)
 13. xcomp (object rel)
     1.  open complement
         1.  When object is a clause with its verb and hire subject from main verb itself
         2.  If you remove ccomp component then sentence become incomplete
         3. Examples
            1. Amit drinks water to run faster
13. cc & conjuc (and , or , )
    1.  Cordinating Conjuction and Conjuction
    2.  Examples
        1.  Amit loves fruits and sweets. Here "and" is cc 
        2.  Fruits and Sweet are in conjuction relationship
14. npadvmod
    1.  noun phrase advmod
    2.  It provide additional details about the main verb
    3.  Entity which is providing additional details is "noun phrase"
    4.  Examples
        1.  yesterday, I played football
        2.  last night, I went to market
15. dative
   1. indirect object
   2. indirect object always bear the result of the main verb
